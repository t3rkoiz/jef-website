42nd Annual Business & Workplace Convention

JEF Convention 2026

"Rebuilding with Purpose: Turning Crisis to Opportunity"

Jamaica's flagship gathering for employers, HR practitioners, and business leaders returns to Kingston on June 11–12, 2026. Two days of keynotes, panels, and practical sessions on resilience, transformation, and the future of work in the Caribbean.

Dates
June 11–12, 2026
Venue
Summit Kingston
Format
In-Person & Virtual
About the Theme

Rebuilding with Purpose

The 2026 convention turns its attention to a question that has shaped Caribbean business for the past five years — how do we move beyond crisis response into sustained renewal? "Rebuilding with Purpose: Turning Crisis to Opportunity" gathers leaders from across Jamaica's business community to share what is actually working in talent retention, productivity, supply-chain resilience, digital transformation, and workplace culture.

Across two days, expect keynote addresses from senior policymakers and global thought-leaders, expert panels on industrial relations and the future of work, and practical workshops designed for HR practitioners, business owners, and decision-makers.

Full programme, speaker lineup, and registration details will be announced closer to the event. Contact the JEF Secretariat to be added to the priority notification list.

Established in 1958, JEF is the sole employers' representative to the ILO in Jamaica and the only organisation of its kind in the region, representing 300+ corporate and individual members.
